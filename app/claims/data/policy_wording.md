# Tern Travel Insurance: Policy Wording

Tern Travel Insurance is a fictional insurer. This wording is the contract with the customer. All amounts are in Australian dollars.

## 1. Plans

| Benefit | Essential | Comprehensive |
|---|---|---|
| Trip cancellation, maximum | $3,000 | $8,000 |
| Trip delay, waiting period | 12 hours | 6 hours |
| Trip delay, maximum | $300 | $600 |
| Baggage, maximum | $1,500 | $3,000 |
| Baggage, limit for each item | $250 | $750 |
| Excess, for cancellation and baggage claims | $100 | $50 |

Trip delay claims have no excess.

## 2. Trip cancellation

We cover the non-refundable trip costs you paid before departure when you cancel for one of these reasons:

- you or a close relative becomes seriously ill or injured, shown by a medical certificate
- a close relative dies, shown by a death certificate
- your departure airport is closed by severe weather

We do not cover a change of plans, work commitments, or an event that was known when the policy was bought.

## 3. Trip delay

We cover a delay when your carrier's departure is late by at least the waiting period for your plan because of a mechanical fault, severe weather, a crew shortage, or a strike announced after the policy was bought.

You need the carrier's written confirmation of the delay **and its cause**, and receipts for what you spent. We pay reasonable accommodation, meals and local transport during the delay, up to the maximum for your plan.

## 4. Baggage

We cover personal items that are lost, stolen or damaged during the trip.

- For a theft, you need a police report made within 24 hours.
- For a loss or damage by a carrier, you need the carrier's report.

We do not cover items left unattended in a public place, or cash. Each item is paid up to the limit for each item on your plan.

## 5. General exclusions

We do not cover incidents before the trip starts or after it ends (except a cancellation under section 2), pre-existing medical conditions, or losses while you were intoxicated.

## 6. How we work out the payment

1. Apply the limit for each item to every baggage item.
2. Take off anything paid by a carrier, a hotel, a tour operator or another insurer.
3. Take off the excess once per claim, where it applies.
4. Pay no more than the maximum for the benefit on your plan.

## 7. Definitions

- **Close relative:** a spouse or partner, parent, child, brother or sister.
- **Unattended:** not in your sight and not locked away, so you could not stop it being taken.
- **Carrier:** an airline, train, bus or ship company that sold you a ticket.
